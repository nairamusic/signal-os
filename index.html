NMC MANIFESTOThe Cartel's founding truth

NMC isn't entertainment. It's DOCUMENTATION. These voices document Pan-African survival, struggle, joy, grief, revolution, and transcendence. They speak for people whose stories aren't told. They honour ancestors while pushing boundaries. They're rooted in tradition while creating the future.

NMC is not a label. It's a cartel — a collective of sovereign artists who chose each other. No one owns anyone here. No advance. No copyright grab. Each artist controls their master recordings. What they share is a manifesto, a frequency, and the question that started it all: "Na lie?"

Transmedia model: Gorillaz / MF DOOM lineage · Wu-Tang crew structure · Fela Kuti political consciousness · AI-transparent — disclosed openly as marketing strength.

THE FORMATIONHow NMC began — Hackney, E8, 2021

2021. A warehouse in Hackney, E8. DJ Na Lie had been running an irregular Friday night called "Signal Lost" — no social media, location dropped by WhatsApp at 10PM, capacity strictly 200. The night existed because he needed somewhere to play tracks that no commercial promoter would book.

The night Boboyi showed up, he'd just arrived from Lagos on a visitor visa. Na Lie played an instrumental built from sampled SARS police radio traffic. Boboyi stood at the decks and freestyled over it for 11 minutes. The 200 people in the room did not move. When he finished, Na Lie said: "Na lie?" Boboyi said: "Ọba kò sọ̀rọ̀ lẹ́ẹ̀mejì." Neither needed a translator.

What followed was not planned. Zooboi came because Boboyi called him. Lyrikal Flame was found through a viral video that Na Lie screenshot at 3AM and sent without comment. Fantasma's track "Madrugada em Luanda" was shared by Boboyi with only: "We need this." Nova Venom arrived without being invited and was already in the recording session when Na Lie got there; nobody has asked how. Asa Brugga attended two sessions, said nothing, sent a finished track two weeks later: "Agua y fuego." Makasi came with a bass guitar and a question: "Can I name the people they won't name?" Pastor Flow entered a Mayfair dinner party and had produced three tracks before anyone realised he was officially part of the Cartel. Rajebu was found through the weight of what he'd survived — 33 scars, London construction, a lifetime of Ajegunle compressed into a baritone.

There was no founding meeting. No term sheets. No pitch deck. NMC formed the way all real things form: people who needed each other found each other, and a frequency was born.

NEW ARTIST INTAKE

Roster checklist

  • Create a draft Artist Bible
  • Define unique archetype and roster role
  • Lock vocal, production and visual rules
  • Record prohibited overlap with existing NMC artists
  • Lock canon only after Naira Music review

NON-NEGOTIABLES

Release safeguards

  • No invented legal credits or ownership shares
  • No live posting without authorised accounts
  • No release below BLACK SIGNAL 95
  • No distribution without real master and artwork
  • No campaign without four human approvals

RECOVERY

If something goes wrong

  • Stop external jobs before retrying
  • Check the Governance audit trail
  • Restore from the latest Data Custody backup
  • Re-run Integrations and Release Preflight
  • Never paste secret tokens into the browser
